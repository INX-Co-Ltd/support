# Words-Rest

**Words-Rest** (Korean title: 문장의 숲) is a mobile app for iPhone and Android by INX Company Limited. Photograph a sentence you came across in a book, a film or on the street, and the app keeps just that sentence as a paper clipping. Each clipping settles onto the canvas of the day you took it, and the forest on your home screen grows as you collect.

There is no account or sign-up. Open the app and start.

## What the App Does

- Finds the sentences in a photo and outlines them. Text recognition runs on your device.
- Saves the sentences you pick as clippings, arranged by date on a calendar and in an album that reads like a picture diary.
- Shows the day's weather on the home screen and remembers where a camera photo was taken — both only if you allow location access.
- Optionally keeps your collection in your own cloud storage — iCloud on iPhone, Google Drive on Android — so you can carry on after you switch phones.

## How Words-Rest Uses Google Drive (Android)

**Keep in Google Drive** is off by default. When you turn it on in Settings › Storage and allow access with your Google account, the app requests only the `drive.appdata` permission: a private app folder in your own Google Drive that only Words-Rest can read and that does not appear in your Drive file list.

- The app uses this folder only to store and restore your own photos, clippings, notes and capture places.
- The data travels directly between your phone and Google over HTTPS. We never receive it, and it is not used for advertising or shared with anyone.
- Turning the option off deletes the copy in Google Drive and revokes the app's access.

Words-Rest's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.
